# Hobbi — Complete Clubs & Explorers Market Research Survey

## Form overview

**Survey title:** Hobbi — Clubs & Explorers Research / استطلاع الأندية والمشاركين

**Purpose:** Understand Lebanese Explorers and club organizers’ existing behavior, pain points, feature needs, booking practices and adoption intent. The survey is not a product signup or a commitment to pay.

**Eligibility:** Adults 18+; voluntary; no automatic email collection. Optional contact only for those who explicitly opt in.

**Flow:** Age/consent screen → choose Explorer or Club → corresponding questions → submit.

**Estimated completion:** Explorer ~6–9 minutes; Club ~8–11 minutes.

## Screening and routing

1. Are you 18 or older and do you agree to participate? (Yes → proceed; No → exit.)
2. Which describes you? (Explorer / Club organizer; if both, complete two responses.)

## Research analysis and decision guide

**Explorers:** Segment by city; track recent participation (E02), current discovery channels (E03), pain points (E04), interests (E05), typical spending (E06), trust (E08), booking approval tolerance (E10), preferred features (E12), and stated adoption interest (E14).

**Clubs:** Compare activity volume and size (C03–C04), current booking workflow (C06), operational pain (C07), time spent (C09), interest in pilot (C13), existing software spend (C15), acceptable monthly Pro price (C16), reasons to pay (C17), and likelihood at $19/month (C18).

**Important:** Intent to try or pay is not evidence of actual adoption or revenue. Follow up with real onboarding, booking, attendance, and paid subscription trials.

**Research ethics:** Share aggregated results, not individual contact details. Do not use optional contact fields unless the respondent consented, and remove unconsented contact information. Test both branches before sending.

**Suggested initial sample:** 50+ Explorers spread across multiple Lebanese cities; 10–15 in-depth club interviews; additional club survey responses; 12 pilot clubs; and a target of three real Pro subscriptions as a later validation milestone. All targets are planning assumptions.
